Manage Campus Locations

Add, edit, or delete locations on your campus map

📌 How to Get Coordinates:

  1. Go to Google Maps
  2. Search for "RNS Institute of Technology" or navigate to your campus
  3. Right-click on the exact location of the building
  4. Click on the coordinates that appear (e.g., "12.90074, 77.51738")
  5. Copy the first number (Latitude) and second number (Longitude)
  6. Paste them in the form below
